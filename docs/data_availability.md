# Data availability

The study combines public institutional and demographic sources with police-record exports supplied for research purposes.

## Public sources

The MUNIC 2023 survey, 2022 Census population data, and municipal geographic reference data are public sources maintained by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE).

## Police records

The 2022–2025 police microdata used in the study are not redistributed in this repository. Access may be subject to institutional authorization and the terms established by the Public Security Secretariat of Pará (SEGUP/CODEC).

## Reproducibility scope

This repository provides:

- final source code;
- parameter files;
- methodological documentation;
- non-identifying aggregate outputs;
- instructions for reconstructing the analysis when authorized source data are available.

The released materials must not be interpreted as authorization to redistribute restricted source data.
